FAQ

Who handles garbage collection? +

Garbage collection in real life is handled by the responsible local authorities and their service providers. TaftClean is currently a classroom prototype and does not send reports to government offices.

How long until my report is reviewed? +

Reports submitted to this prototype begin with “Submitted.” You can demonstrate later steps using the tracking page's demo status controls. No official review time is guaranteed.

Can I report without a photo? +

Yes. A photo is helpful but optional. Required details are your email, garbage type, description, date observed, and street or barangay.
