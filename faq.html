FAQ

Who handles garbage collection? +

Garbage collection in real life is handled by the responsible local authorities and their service providers. TaftClean is currently a classroom prototype and does not send reports to government offices.

How long until my report is reviewed? +

Reports submitted to this prototype begin with “Submitted.” You can demonstrate later steps using the tracking page's demo status controls. No official review time is guaranteed.

Can I report without a photo? +

Yes. A photo is helpful but optional. Required details are your email, garbage type, description, date observed, and street or barangay.

Can I check my report without an account? +

Yes. Keep the reference number displayed after submission and enter it on the Track page. For now, reports only exist in this browser.

Who can see my email or photo? +

This local demo does not send reports to an external server. The public dashboard does not display emails. Browser data is still accessible to people using the same browser profile, so avoid real personal information. Read the privacy policy.

How can I avoid submitting the same report twice? +

Save your reference number and use the Track page first. The prototype also blocks obviously repeated reports in the same browser for a short period.
