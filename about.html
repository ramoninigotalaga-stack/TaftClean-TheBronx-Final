About TaftClean

See Trash? Report it, Don't Step Over it.

TaftClean is a student-designed citizen reporting prototype focused on uncollected garbage around Taft Avenue. It gives people a simple way to document a concern, receive a reference number, and view report progress.

Our Goal

Make garbage reporting understandable and accessible, even for first-time visitors. The intended future workflow is to help authorities receive structured location details and update residents as action is taken. This classroom version does not contact any agency.

How It Works

01

Document the problem

Enter the garbage type, date, and a short description. Add a photo if you have one.

02

Pinpoint the location

Describe the barangay, street segment, and optional landmark. No GPS is required.

03

Save & track

Submit to receive a unique reference number. Use it to follow the report’s progress.

Who is this for?

Residents, commuters, students, and anyone who notices improperly managed waste near Taft. We designed readable labels, keyboard navigation, simple language, clear error feedback, and a mobile-friendly layout.
