Contact Us

TaftClean is a student-built prototype by WEBDEV Group The Bronx.

For prototype questions

For class-related questions, contact The Bronx through your course communication channel. For a code-related issue, use the project GitHub Issues page (GitHub sign-in required to post). This student prototype has no official phone line or government support inbox.

For an actual garbage concern

Please use your local barangay or city government's official reporting channels. Submissions here are not sent to authorities.

For help using this site

Try the FAQ, read How It Works, or review our Privacy Policy.
