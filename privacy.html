Classroom prototype policies

Privacy Policy & Reporting Guidelines

This website is a student demonstration. It is not connected to a city office.

What information is collected?

The report form asks for an email, garbage subtype, description, date observed, and street or barangay. You may optionally add a landmark, estimated pile size, estimated number of bags, and photo.

Where is data stored?

Text details and status are saved in your browser's localStorage. Photos, when available, are saved in the browser's IndexedDB. No database, government office, or automatic email notification receives them. Sample reports are preloaded for demonstration.

Who can access it?

The dashboard intentionally hides email addresses. However, data stored locally is not encrypted or protected from other people using the same browser profile. Clear the browser's site data to remove saved reports. Do not enter real sensitive or identifying information for class demonstrations.

How long is information kept?

It stays on this browser until its site data is cleared or the browser removes it. There is no centralized account or retention system.

Reporting Guidelines

  • Submit only genuine or fictional classroom example observations, not harassment or accusations against individuals.
  • Describe the location clearly, without sharing someone else's private address or personal details.
  • Use JPG/PNG photos up to 5MB; avoid faces, number plates, or other identifying details.
  • Keep your reference number; check the tracking page before submitting an obvious duplicate.
  • This prototype blocks certain duplicate or rapid submissions in the same browser, but it cannot stop all abuse.

Prototype Terms

Data and statuses on this site are for class demonstration only. A status labeled Resolved does not mean an actual government cleanup happened. Do not rely on this website for urgent or official complaints.
